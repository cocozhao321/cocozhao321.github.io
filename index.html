01 — Focus

What it takes to trust an agent

A demo agent is easy. An agent a business can rely on needs evidence it works, a strict limit on what it can access, and guardrails for when things go wrong. That is the part I own.

  1. 01

    Evaluate

    Evaluation is the center of a trustworthy agent ecosystem, and I lead it for our multi-agent platform. A release gate in CI/CD scores every agent against ground truth before it ships. In production, a critic agent and daily signal models score live answers into one reliability score, and red-teaming finds failures before customers do.

    • LLM-as-judge
    • Release gates
    • Red-teaming
  2. 02

    Govern access

    I founded and launched the MCP server that gives agents one secure, auditable way to reach highly sensitive customer data, with authentication, fine-grained authorization and data-classification controls built in. Its reusable framework cut other teams' build time for a new MCP server from two months to two weeks.

    • MCP
    • AuthN / AuthZ
    • Data classification
  3. 03

    Guard

    I build the guardrails that run in production: prompt-injection defense, PII detection and scrubbing at >99.9% accuracy, and hallucination monitoring. I've taken AI products through multiple security reviews, and I'm adding formal security training as an NYU Cyber Fellow.

    • Prompt-injection defense
    • PII protection
    • Threat modeling

03 — About

From neuroscience labs to production AI agents

Hi, I'm Coco. I grew up in Wenzhou, China, moved to Philadelphia at 16, and graduated Magna Cum Laude from the University of Pennsylvania with a double major in Computer Science and Cognitive Science.

At Amazon I work on the parts of AI agents that decide whether people can trust them: how agents are evaluated, what data they can reach, and what stops them when something goes wrong. The data involved is highly sensitive customer data, so security and governance are part of every design.

Before Amazon I co-founded ForeverBrainTech, a behavioral-assessment startup that grew to 100k+ users and was acquired in 2022, and spent four years doing neuroimaging research at UPenn, Princeton and Penn Medicine, which led to 8 peer-reviewed papers.

The thread through all of it is measurement. My research asked whether a reading of the brain could be trusted, and my startup built behavioral assessments people could rely on. Evaluating an AI agent is the same problem with a different subject.
